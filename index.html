AEPril's Ears — Support

AEPril's Ears (Ears in Armor) captures tamper-evident audio recordings of industrial-safety conversations and preserves them under a cryptographic chain of custody. It is distributed privately to participating organizations.

Contact support

For questions, problems, or support requests, email balkin.4@osu.edu. We aim to respond within two business days.

What to include in a support request

  • Your name and organization
  • Device model and iOS version (Settings > General > About)
  • The app version and build number (shown in the app's Settings tab)
  • What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead

Common questions

  • I can't sign in. Accounts are provisioned by the program administrator — the app does not offer self-signup. If your temporary password has expired or you've forgotten your credentials, contact support to have them reissued.
  • I forgot my PIN. Contact support. For security reasons the PIN cannot be recovered; support will walk you through re-provisioning.
  • My recording hasn't uploaded. Recordings made offline upload automatically the next time the app is open with an internet connection. If a recording appears stuck for more than a day, contact support with the approximate date and length of the recording.
