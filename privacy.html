Ears in Armor — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 August 2026

Ears in Armor ("the app") is a workplace-safety audio-evidence tool operated for a closed industrial pilot program. This policy describes what the app collects and how that information is handled. The app is available by invitation only.

What the app collects

  • Audio recordings you choose to make. Recording starts only when you press record. Recordings may contain private, confidential, or personally identifiable information discussed during workplace-safety conversations.
  • Account information. The email address used for your invited account.
  • Device and security information. Device identifiers and cryptographic attestations used to verify that uploads come from an enrolled device.
  • Diagnostic metadata. Technical measurements about recording sessions (durations, counts, error codes). Diagnostic records never include recording content, transcripts, or location.

How recordings are protected

  • Recordings are encrypted on your device at the moment of capture, before storage or transmission.
  • Recordings cannot be played back inside the app. Decryption is possible only through a controlled, multi-person approval process in secured infrastructure, with every access cryptographically logged.
  • Successfully uploaded recordings are removed from the device.

How information is used

Recordings exist to support workplace-safety programs, incident investigation, and, where required, legal proceedings. Diagnostic metadata is used solely to keep the service working reliably. Nothing the app collects is used for advertising, profiling, or marketing, and nothing is sold or rented to anyone.

Sharing

Information is shared only with the pilot program's operating organizations under confidentiality obligations, with infrastructure providers that store encrypted data on our behalf, and where required by law or legal process.

Retention

Recordings are retained under evidence-retention rules for the pilot program (up to seven years) and cannot be altered during that period. Diagnostic metadata is retained for 90 days. Account records are retained for the duration of the pilot.

Your choices

Recording is always user-initiated. You may stop participating in the pilot at any time by contacting the program administrator; your account will be disabled. Because recordings are evidence, deletion during the retention period may not be possible.
